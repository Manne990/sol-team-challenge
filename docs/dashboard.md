# Dashboard metric policy

Dashboard evidence is generated by one organization-scoped request and carries
its UTC `generatedAt` instant. Refresh replaces the prior response; values are
not incrementally accumulated.

- Open pipeline includes non-archived deals whose status is `open`. Amounts are
  never combined across currencies.
- Stage distribution applies the same open-deal rule and groups by pipeline
  stage and currency.
- Won/lost trend includes non-archived outcome deals updated during the prior
  90 × 24 hours. The update timestamp is the current V1 outcome evidence.
- Overdue tasks are open tasks due strictly before `generatedAt`. Upcoming
  tasks are open tasks due from `generatedAt` through the next 7 × 24 hours.
- Closing soon includes open deals with UTC expected-close dates from the
  generated date through 30 days later, inclusive.
- Stale accounts are non-archived companies with no activity at or after the
  30-day cutoff; companies with no recorded activity are stale.
- Recent activity is the latest eight entries ordered by occurrence time and
  stable ID.

Metric links encode the same status, stage, date, due-state, or activity cutoff
used by the aggregate. Empty organizations return zeros and deliberate empty
states rather than synthetic values.
